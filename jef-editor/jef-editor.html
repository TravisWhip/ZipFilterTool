Shapes (Select tool)

Pick a shape (R O L T) and drag to draw; click for a default-size shape. Click a shape to select it, drag to move. Drag the corner/edge handles to resize, the round handle to rotate, the center dot to move. Shapes snap to the edges and centers of other shapes, the design and the 0" lines (blue guides).
Shift keep proportions / 15° · Ctrl resize from center · Alt no snapping · Shift+click add to selection · drag on empty space to box-select · arrows nudge (Shift = bigger step) · Ctrl+C/V/D copy/paste/duplicate · Del delete · Space/right-drag pan · wheel zoom.

Measure tool

Press M. Drag between two points to measure; the ends snap to edges and centers (Shift = straight across / up-down / 45°, Alt = no snapping). Or click one element, then another to measure the gap between them — or all four margins if one is inside the other. Click a measurement to select it, drag its ends to adjust, Del to remove. Measurements aren't saved in the JEF file.

Stitch points (Points tool)

Click a point to select · Shift+click/drag to add · drag to move · double-click to add a stitch · Del delete · I insert · J stitch/jump · arrows nudge 0.1 mm. Shapes become editable points after To stitches.
With the Select tool, each block of stitches between jumps is a stitch run: click it to select, then move, resize, rotate, copy or delete it like a shape.
