Shapes (Select tool)

Pick a shape (R O L T) and drag to draw; click for a 20 mm shape. Click a shape to select it, drag to move. Drag the corner/edge handles to resize, the round handle to rotate, the center dot to move. Shapes snap to the edges and centers of other shapes, the design and the 0" lines (blue guides).
Shift keep proportions / 15° · Ctrl resize from center · Alt no snapping · Shift+click add to selection · drag on empty space to box-select · arrows nudge 0.5 mm (Shift 5 mm) · Ctrl+C/V/D copy/paste/duplicate · Del delete · Space/right-drag pan · wheel zoom.

Stitch points (Points tool)

Click a point to select · Shift+click/drag to add · drag to move · double-click to add a stitch · Del delete · I insert · J stitch/jump · arrows nudge 0.1 mm. Shapes become editable points after To stitches.
